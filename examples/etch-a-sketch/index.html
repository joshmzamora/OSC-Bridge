Zig-sim Etch-a-sketch

By Yonatan Rozin

Zig Sim free required (iOS tested) w/ 2D touch, accel and touch radius data enabled.

Use this computer's IP address and port 4242 in Zig Sim settings.

Use phone screen to draw, shake phone to clear!

ctrl-F (or cmd-F) to toggle fullscreen (esc to exit).
ctrl-R (or cmd-R) to refresh page.
ctrl-E (or cmd-E) to open live sketch files folder.
